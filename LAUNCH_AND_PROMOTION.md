## Responsible outreach
- Send only to contacts who would welcome the invitation. Ask friends and family to share if they genuinely enjoy it.
- Post in communities permitting game sharing. Do not impersonate players or buy fake reviews.
- Invite voluntary feedback, not children's personal information.
- Never claim prizes, income, or incentives that are not actually provided.

## Five-question playtest
1. Which device and mode did you try? (Do not collect names.)
2. Could you play without scrolling?
3. Were controls clear and responsive?
4. What was the most fun or frustrating part?
5. Would you choose to play again? Why?
