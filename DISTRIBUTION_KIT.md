## Full description
Block Rush: Worlds & Wonders is a colourful, free HTML5 block puzzle game. Choose from six different modes: Junior for younger players; Classic for endless strategy; Arcade for time-limited action; Daily for a new local-date challenge; Adventure to unlock progressive missions; and Zen to play at your own pace.

Select one of three available shapes, place it onto the 8×8 board, and fill rows or columns to clear them and earn points. The game stores personal progress and high scores locally in the browser. No account, download, or payments are required.
